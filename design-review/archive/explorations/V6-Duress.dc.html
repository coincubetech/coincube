Duress Mode

What duress mode does

Unlocking any one Cube with your duress PIN wipes every Cube on this device and signals Connect to lock your entire account — this locks all of your Cubes, not just the one you opened. The device then shows a dead-end screen until you clear duress from another trusted device.

Duress mode is enabled

Your duress PIN is armed on every Cube on this device. Entering it at any Cube's unlock screen triggers a duress wipe and locks your account.
Duress mode is enabled on your account

It was set up on another device, so it is not armed on this one — entering your duress PIN here will not trigger a wipe. Manage duress mode from the device where you enabled it.

You'll set

  • A duress PIN — one you don't use to unlock any of your Cubes.
  • An all-clear passphrase — at least 12 characters, used to unlock your account from a trusted device.
  • A duress recovery-kit password — covers all your Cubes.
  • An unlock delay: 24h · 48h · 7d · 30d · 90d.

Set up a Cube Recovery Kit for each Cube

Without a Cube Recovery Kit, a duress wipe is irreversible from Connect. Seed-only Cubes need their Master Seed Phrase backed up; Cubes with a Vault need both the Master Seed Phrase and the Vault Wallet Descriptor. Finish the Recovery Kit for each of your Vault Cubes before enabling duress mode.

This password applies to all your Cubes. If you ever enter it on a recovery screen, the entire account enters duress.
